About Us

Luxe Cotton Touch — crafted with care

Luxe Cotton Touch started as a simple idea: everyone deserves a bedsheet that actually feels good against the skin, without paying showroom prices for it. We work directly with cotton mills and small weaving units, checking every batch for weave quality, softness and colourfastness before it reaches you.

Every order is packed by hand and shipped with the same care we'd want for our own home. No middlemen, no unnecessary markup — just honest cotton, honestly priced.

  • 100% pure cotton, no synthetic blends
  • Pre-shrunk & colourfast fabric
  • Direct from source, fair pricing
  • Pan-India shipping
